2. Description of Service

SolHerd is a cattle breeding intelligence tool that provides:

  • Breed data and Expected Progeny Difference (EPD) reference information. EPDs are published by each breed association on that breed's own genetic base and are not directly comparable between breeds; the App applies no across-breed adjustment
  • A breeding pair calculator for educational comparison purposes
  • Cattle market data sourced from public government databases. Live price reports cover the United States (USDA) and Brazil (IBGE) only; other regions show World Bank production indices and a link to the publisher
  • On-device herd management and record-keeping functionality

The App is distributed exclusively via the Solana Seeker dApp Store and uses Sign In With Solana (SIWS) for identity verification.
