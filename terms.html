3. Description of Service

The App is a mobile application for Android devices that displays cattle breed information, breed-average EPDs (Expected Progeny Differences), hypothetical bull-cow pairing scores, estimated calf values, and livestock market data sourced from government and public third-party APIs. The App is provided solely for entertainment, novelty, informational, and educational purposes.

The App’s features include:

  • Cattle breed encyclopedia with profiles for 20+ breeds
  • Breed-average EPD tables and economic index comparisons
  • Hypothetical bull-cow pairing score calculator
  • Estimated calf value calculator based on market data and breed factors
  • Live livestock market data from USDA and other government APIs
  • Crossbreeding heterosis reference tables
  • Gestation date calculator
  • Configurable market price notification reminders
