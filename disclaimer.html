3. Breed Data and EPD Accuracy Disclaimer

Cattle breed information and Expected Progeny Difference (EPD) data displayed in the App is based on publicly published breed-average figures from breed associations and may:

  • Be outdated — Breed-average EPDs are updated annually by breed associations; the App may not reflect the most current evaluation
  • Be inaccurate — Figures may contain transcription errors, rounding differences, or discrepancies from source data
  • Not represent individual animals — Breed averages do not predict the genetic merit of any specific animal; individual animal EPDs require breed association lookup and/or genomic testing
  • Vary by evaluation run — Breed associations periodically recalculate EPDs; historical figures may differ from current evaluations
  • Not be comparable across breeds — EPDs from different breed evaluations (e.g., Angus vs. Hereford) use different base populations and are NOT directly comparable unless adjusted by a multi-breed evaluation system

4. Pairing Score and Price Estimate Disclaimer

Pairing scores and price estimates displayed in SolHerd are purely hypothetical, illustrative, and for entertainment purposes only. They are NOT predictions, recommendations, appraisals, or valuations. They must NOT be used for actual breeding decisions, livestock purchases, sales, or any financial decisions.

Pairing scores are simplified calculations based on breed-average EPDs and general heterosis estimates. They do not account for:

  • Individual animal genetics, genomic test results, or actual EPD values
  • Actual pedigree, lineage, inbreeding coefficients, or ancestry
  • Animal health, fertility, structural soundness, or temperament
  • Environmental conditions, management practices, or nutrition
  • Epigenetic factors, gene interactions, or non-additive genetic effects

Price estimates are rough approximations based on publicly available market data and general industry factors. They do not constitute appraisals, valuations, or market forecasts. Actual livestock values depend on innumerable factors that the App cannot assess.

5. Market Data Accuracy Disclaimer

Livestock market data is sourced from government and public APIs (USDA Agricultural Marketing Service, IBGE SIDRA, Eurostat, and other sources) and:

  • May be delayed — Market reports may be hours, days, or weeks old depending on the data source and reporting schedule
  • May be inaccurate — Third-party data sources may provide incorrect data due to API errors, reporting delays, or data feed issues
  • May be incomplete — Not all auction markets, regions, or livestock categories are represented
  • May be entirely unavailable — API services may experience downtime, rate limiting, maintenance, or discontinuation
  • May differ from actual local prices — Livestock prices vary significantly by location, quality, lot size, buyer relationships, and market conditions
  • Are not audited or verified — We do not independently verify the accuracy of data from our third-party sources

Under no circumstances should you rely on market data displayed by SolHerd for trading, investment, livestock purchasing, selling, or any financial decisions.

By using SolHerd, you acknowledge that you understand and accept the following risks inherent in cattle breeding and livestock markets:

6.1 Biological variability

Cattle genetics and breeding outcomes are inherently variable and unpredictable. No tool, app, algorithm, or formula can guarantee specific breeding results. Even well-planned matings can produce unexpected outcomes.

6.2 Market volatility

Livestock markets are volatile. Cattle prices fluctuate based on supply, demand, weather, feed costs, disease outbreaks, trade policy, consumer preferences, and numerous other factors beyond anyone’s control or prediction.

6.3 Regulatory risk

Agricultural regulations, animal health requirements, import/export restrictions, environmental regulations, and trade agreements vary by jurisdiction and change frequently. Changes in regulation could materially affect livestock values and operations.

6.4 Disease and health risk

Livestock are subject to diseases, parasites, injuries, and health conditions that can dramatically affect value, productivity, and viability. The App provides no health information, diagnostic tools, or veterinary guidance.

6.5 Environmental risk

Drought, flooding, extreme temperatures, wildfire, and other environmental events can devastate livestock operations and market conditions.

6.6 Total loss risk

The value of any livestock may decline to zero. Animals may die, become unsaleable, or lose all productive value. You could lose the entire value of your livestock holdings.
