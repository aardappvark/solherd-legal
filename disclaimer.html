6. Data Accuracy Disclaimer

All data displayed in SolHerd, including but not limited to breed information, EPD values, market data, and herd calculations, may contain errors, inaccuracies, or omissions. Specifically:

  • EPD Data: Breed-average EPD values are sourced from publicly published summaries and may not reflect current breed averages. EPDs are published on each breed's own genetic base and are not directly comparable between breeds; the pairing calculator combines them without any across-breed adjustment, so cross-breed scores are indicative only. EPD values are updated periodically by breed associations and may change. Individual animal EPDs vary significantly from breed averages.
  • Market Data: Sourced from government and institutional databases that may have reporting delays, data revisions, or errors. Prices are indicative only and do not represent offers to buy or sell.
  • Breeding Calculator: Calculations are based on simplified genetic models and breed-average data. Actual breeding outcomes are influenced by many factors not captured by the calculator.
  • Breed Information: Breed descriptions and characteristics are general summaries and may not reflect the full diversity within a breed or the latest breed developments.

You should independently verify all data before relying on it for any purpose.
